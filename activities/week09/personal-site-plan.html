Your Personal Site Plan

Before we develop your personal project, we need to do some planning. We started this project by preparing our purpose with the elevator pitch. Now you will create a site plan for your personal site using this template: Site Plan Template.

Activity Directions

  1. Content and wireframes

    The site plan document is similar to the site plan from the rafting site, with one added section: Content! For the rafting site, you were provided with content, but for your personal site you will need to generate all of the content (text and media) for your site. Your plan is not complete without all the necessary content to build your site.

    You will also be required to create two wireframes: one for the homepage, and one for the child pages. Create wireframes with any tool you would like even pencil and paper.

    2. Personal Site Requirements

    As you plan, consider the overall requirements for your personal site:

    1. Your personal site must consist of a home page and at least two child pages.
    2. The website must be hosted on the web.
    3. Each page of your website must satisfy the following requirements:
      1. Significant textual content consisting of appropriate content for your audience so that they can understand the meaning and message of this site.
      2. Two or more images that relates to the content of the page (in addition to the logo image).
      3. Working links to your two child pages.
      4. Be laid out and styled according to your site plan wireframes.
      5. Be visually appealing and easily readable.

  2. Purpose and Audience

    In 2–3 sentences, write about the purpose of your site and who your intented audience will be. This information should match up with the information in your elevator pitch.

  3. Logo

    Create a logo for your site. You may use AI, or other online tools to help you create your logo.

  4. Colors and Fonts

    Choose a color scheme with at least 4 colors. List one or more fonts that you think will work well for the site typography.

  5. Submit

    Submit your completed site plan document (please save it as: lastname-site-plan.docx). Make sure your plan is complete and detailed well enough that anyone could use your plan to build your site.
