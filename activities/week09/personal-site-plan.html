Your Personal Site Plan

Before we develop your personal project, we need to do some planning. We started this project by preparing our purpose with the elevator pitch. Now you will create a site plan for your personal site using this template: Site Plan Templage.

Activity Directions

  1. As you look at the new site plan template, you will notice it is very similar to the last one. It does have a new section though: Content. For the rafting site, you were provided with content, but you will need to generate all of the content (text and media) for your site.

    You will also be required to create three wireframes: one for each page of your site. Creating wireframes will be covered at a later time.

    We will have some activities designed to help you complete your site plan, but much of it you will need to do on your own. The completed site plan for your personal site is due at the end of Week 9. This gives you three weeks to complete it. Don't wait until the beginning of Week 9 to start!

    2. Information about your personal site that might be helpful to know as you start the site plan.

    By the end of this course:

    1. The personal site must consist of a home page and at least two child pages.
    2. The website must be hosted on the web.
    3. Each page of your Web site must satisfy the following requirements:
      1. Significant textual content consisting of appropriate content for your audience so that they can understand the meaning and message of this site.
      2. At least one image that relates to the content of the page.
      3. Working links to your two child pages.
      4. Be laid out and styled according to your site plan wireframes.
      5. Be visually appealing and easily readable.

  2. Purpose and Audience

    In 2–3 sentences, write the purpose statement from your elevator pitch and write about your intended audience.

  3. Logo

    Choose a logo images for your personal site. You can create one if you'd like. Sites like Canva.com or Adobe Logo Maker or freelogodesign.org can be options if you don't make yours from scratch.

  4. Colors and Fonts

    Choose a color scheme and one or more fonts that you think will work well and enter the information into the plan's HTML and CSS.

    Commit and push your file additions and changes to GitHub.

  5. Submit

    Remember, we don't have an index.html or home page inside our personal site project folder yet. So you have to type in the site-plan.html at the end of the URL web address and select Enter to get it to open.

    Something like this: your-account.github.io/wwd130/personal-folder/site-plan.html

    Your site plan should then come up. Use that URL to submit.

    Submit the GitHub link to your site-plan.html page to I-Learn.
