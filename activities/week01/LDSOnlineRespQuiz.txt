LDS Online Responsibilities Quiz

1. According to Elder M. Russell Ballard, why is the Internet a powerful tool for sharing the gospel?
A) It replaces the need for missionaries
B) It allows members to argue gospel topics with others
C) It enables individuals to reach large audiences with their testimonies
D) It keeps Church teachings secret

Correct Answer: C) It enables individuals to reach large audiences with their testimonies

2. What is one simple way the article suggests members can share their testimonies online?
A) Posting Church manuals in full on social media
B) Creating controversial religious debates in forums
C) Bearing testimony and sharing gospel experiences on blogs or social platforms
D) Uploading only General Conference talks without commentary

Correct Answer: C) Bearing testimony and sharing gospel experiences on blogs or social platforms

3. What principle does Elder Ballard emphasize when using the Internet to share the gospel?
A) Avoid using the Church's name online
B) Share personal, authentic experiences and testimonies
C) Only quote scriptures without explanation
D) Focus solely on doctrine without personal feelings

Correct Answer: B) Share personal, authentic experiences and testimonies

4. How can sharing the gospel online bless others, according to the article?
A) It replaces traditional Church meetings
B) It allows others to criticize the Church
C) It opens hearts and inspires others to learn about Jesus Christ
D) It guarantees viral popularity

Correct Answer: C) It opens hearts and inspires others to learn about Jesus Christ
