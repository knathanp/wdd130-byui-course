Multiple Choice Quiz: Evaluating Online Information and Understanding Truth

1. What is one reason the World Health Organization declared an "infodemic"?
A) The internet was too slow to deliver news
B) There was a shortage of trustworthy websites
C) Misinformation is readily available online
D) Students were not using social media enough
Answer: C

2. According to Elder David A. Bednar, what is a potential danger of AI companions?
A) They are usually expensive
B) They lack emotional intelligence
C) They can replace meaningful human relationships
D) They do not work without the internet
Answer: C

3. What is a recommended way to evaluate if a website is reputable?
A) Check if it uses a lot of images
B) Confirm it has many pop-up ads
C) Look at the domain name or URL
D) See if it appears first on a search engine
Answer: C

4. What lesson is taught by the poem about the blind men and the elephant?
A) Truth is always hidden
B) Everyone’s perspective is wrong
C) One part of the truth does not represent the whole truth
D) Observation always leads to correct conclusions
Answer: C

5. According to BBB tips, what is a red flag of a fake website?
A) It has a .org domain
B) It uses subdomains to mimic trusted sites
C) It loads too quickly
D) It has too much content
Answer: B

6. What does Elder Bednar emphasize as essential for spiritual growth?
A) Avoiding all technology
B) Using AI frequently
C) Focused, sustained, and righteous work
D) Relying on others for answers
Answer: C

7. What kind of advertisements suggest a website might be biased?
A) Ads that are unrelated to the content
B) Ads that offer sales and discounts
C) Ads promoting one point of view
D) Ads that use cookies
Answer: C

8. How does Elder Bednar describe AI's potential if misused?
A) A useful school tool
B) A fun distraction
C) A threat to moral agency
D) A programming challenge
Answer: C

9. What is a key sign of a legitimate online store, according to BBB?
A) A long FAQ section
B) Many flashy animations
C) Clear contact information and shop policies
D) Use of stock images
Answer: C

10. What does Elder Bednar say about the Holy Ghost and AI?
A) AI can replace the Holy Ghost in some situations
B) The Holy Ghost helps us understand how to use AI properly
C) The Holy Ghost and AI are unrelated
D) AI is more reliable than the Holy Ghost
Answer: B
