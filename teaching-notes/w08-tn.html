Week 08 Teacher Notes

Student Expectations At Home

  • Add an ice.html page to your portal that shares the same CSS. Link to all ice challenges from the ice.html page. Complete the Portal Page assignment.
  • Complete Image Gallery Issues ICE challenge (if not completed in class)
  • Complete Signup Form Design ICE challenge (if not completed in class)

Class Lesson Suggestions

  • Review:
    Review html/css concepts that will be needed to complete the ice challenges. The final ice challenge this week should require them to utilize all the concepts they have learned.
  • Introduce ICE challenge: Image Gallery Issues
    Demonsrate how to unzip the starter files and save them to the wdd130/ice folder. Explain the expectation of the challenge. Emphasize how big the offending files are, and have a short discussion about bits and bytes and file sizes. Demonstrate using a photo editor. Then give students half the class to work together and try to complete the challenge assignment. Visit with students to assess their progress, give feedback, and give them scaffolding as needed.
  • Introduce ICE challenge: Signup Form Design
    Demonsrate how to unzip the starter files and save them to the wdd130/ice folder. Explain the expectation of the challenge. Then give students half the class to work together and try to complete the challenge assignment. Visit with students to assess their progress, give feedback. Students should try to complete the challenge without scaffolding from you.

Week 08 Goals

  • Learn how to use a photo editor to optimize files for the web.
  • Practice using the same CSS for mulitple pages with the portal ice page.
  • Practice all the basic HTML and CSS skills in one challenge.
  • Complete the portal page assignment.
