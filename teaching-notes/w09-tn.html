Week 09 Teacher Notes

Student Expectations At Home

  • Starting with the the elevator pitch idea chosen in class, make a site plan with a wireframe design, and create content (text and images) for the site to be built.
  • Read and watch information on how to recognize truth online. Take the quiz.

Class Lesson Suggestions

  • Elevator Pitch Group Activity:
    Have each student decide on the topic for their personal site. Have them quickly create an elevator pitch using AI to help. Then, in small groups have each person give their elevator speech to the group and get feedback from the the other members of the group.
  • Review Personal Site Plan Assignment:
    Walk through the expectations for the Personal Site Plan. Review wireframes and elements of a good web page layout. Talk about creative resources to help decide on a color scheme and choose fonts and make a logo. Stress the importance of creating the content for the site, all the text and images. Tell them to create their site plan with enough details that someone else would be able to make their site the way they wanted it. Give students time in class to work one their site plans.

Week 09 Goals

  • Make a complete and detailed site plan starting with an idea for a site.
  • Think about truth online, creating truth online, and thinking about what is truth.
