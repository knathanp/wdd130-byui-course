Week 07 Teacher Notes

Student Expectations At Home

  • Follow the portal plan and style the html with CSS (Progress Check 300)
  • Complete Country Flags with Grid ICE challenge (if not completed in class)
  • Complete CSS Positioning ICE challenge (if not completed in class)
  • Watch a video about backend development. Take the quiz.

Class Lesson Suggestions

  • Review:
    Review html/css concepts that will be needed to complete the ice challenges, encourage students to play the grid games if they have not.
  • Introduce ICE challenge: Country Flags with Grid
    Demonsrate how to unzip the starter files and save them to the wdd130/ice folder. Explain the expectation of the challenge. Then give students half the class to work together and try to complete the challenge assignment. Visit with students to assess their progress, give feedback, and give them scaffolding as needed.
  • Introduce ICE challenge: CSS Positioning
    Demonsrate how to unzip the starter files and save them to the wdd130/ice folder. Explain the expectation of the challenge. Then give students half the class to work together and try to complete the challenge assignment. Visit with students to assess their progress, give feedback, and give them scaffolding as needed.
  • Class Discussion: Have a short discussion about being a backend developer and how HTML and CSS fit with that career

Week 07 Goals

  • Practice positioning elements with CSS Grid and getting used to the many properties.
  • Practice styling a page according to a planned design with the portal page.
  • Think about careers for Web Development. Specifically Backend Development.
