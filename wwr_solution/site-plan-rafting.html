Overview

Purpose

The purpose of this site is to increase our outreach as a company. The main focus will be to get the word out about what services we offer and how they can make purchases and get to our physical location.

Audience

The site will be reaching out mainly to the middle class who are in good enough pysical condition to participate in white water rafting. Mainly ages 15-50. They will be looking for a break from their day to day lives to participate in an exciting activity for the weekend. They want to find something that is affordable but still feels like a very unique experience that they will remember for decades. Because of the wide variety of people who will be using our website we want it to be availible from all kinds of devices from Desktops to Laptops and phones.
